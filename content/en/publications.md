The QUALIRAD paper came out of the Health with Data course, from a real problem at the Hospital das Clínicas of UFPE, and was built together with the hospital's medical physicists and clinical engineers. The two 2023 papers were developed during Scientific Initiation at the Federal Institute of Sertão Pernambucano and presented in person at the congress. All of them are applied research, starting from real data and concrete questions in health and environmental contexts.
